About the Studio

Simplicity.
Sustainability.
Serenity.

Matteo Valois is a design studio creating architecture that speaks to the soul. We believe in the power of simplicity — using form, light, and material to craft spaces that offer tranquility and clarity in a complex world.

Our approach is collaborative and client-focused, resulting in buildings that are both timeless and deeply personal.

Our Philosophy

Space as a
Form of Silence

We design with intention. Every line, material, and ray of light serves a purpose: to create a harmonious and humane environment. By stripping away the non-essential, we arrive at an architecture that is honest, enduring, and profoundly peaceful.

Honest Materials

Concrete, timber, and stone — chosen for truth, not trend.

Living Light

Every aperture is placed to move with the sun throughout the day.

Zero Waste Design

Structures designed to give back as much as they take from the earth.
